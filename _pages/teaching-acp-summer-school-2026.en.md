    <h2>About the summer school</h2>
    <p class="award-detail__lead">The school is organized by the Association for Constraint Programming (ACP) and hosted by the Computer Science Elite Talent Cultivation Base of Jilin University, under the theme <strong>CP: Explanation, Verification and Applications</strong>. Nine invited speakers — Emir Demirović, Tias Guns, Alexey Ignatiev, Ciaran McCreesh, Jakob Nordström, Helmut Simonis, Peter J. Stuckey, Guido Tack, and Allen Z. Zhong — gave lectures, and the program also featured a hackathon.</p>
